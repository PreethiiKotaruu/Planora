# Planora Project Charter

## Product Name

Planora

## Tagline

Turn confusing group chats into one clear plan.

## Product Description

Planora is a private group-planning platform where friends create groups, invite members, suggest activities, vote on options, discuss plans, and finalize decisions together.

## Problem

Group plans are commonly organized through scattered messages on applications such as WhatsApp, Instagram, and iMessage.

Suggestions become buried, voting is informal, and members may not clearly understand the final decision.

## Proposed Solution

Planora provides one organized location where group members can:

1. Create a plan
2. Submit suggestions
3. Vote on options
4. Discuss the plan
5. Finalize a shared decision

## Target Users

### Group Organizer

Creates groups, invites friends, manages polls, and finalizes decisions.

### Group Member

Joins groups, adds suggestions, votes, comments, and views finalized plans.

## Product Principles

- Private by default
- Simple to understand
- Mobile-friendly
- Useful without AI
- Improved through real-user feedback
- AI enhances rather than controls the experience

## Project Duration

Eight weeks

## Main Goal

Design, develop, test, deploy, and release Planora to real users.

## Success Criteria

Planora will be considered successful when:

- The application is deployed through a public URL.
- A user can complete the full registration-to-final-decision workflow.
- At least 10 people are invited to test the application.
- Feedback is collected from at least 5 users.
- The most important usability issues discovered during testing are fixed.
- The application works on both desktop and mobile screen sizes.
- An optional AI Smart Planner feature is evaluated after the MVP is stable.
